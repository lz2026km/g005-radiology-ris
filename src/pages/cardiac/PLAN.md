# Module 5.6 & 5.8: CV Database, QC & Operations — Pages

## 5.6 CV Database & QC (25 pts)
- `CvDatabasePage.tsx` — CV imaging database: case browser with filters (modality, anatomy, date range, diagnosis), DICOM SR viewer, structured data display (CAD-RADS, EF, pressures), data export CSV/JSON, cohort builder for research queries, lesion tracking over serial exams, RECIST-like measurement tracking
- `CvQcPage.tsx` — CV quality control dashboard: modality-specific QC metrics (CCTA image quality, MR adequacy, echo completeness), contrast-to-noise ratio tracking, motion score analysis, diagnostic confidence ratings, peer review integration, accreditation readiness (ICAEL/ICACTL/SCCT), report completeness checklists, turnaround time metrics

## 5.8 CV Operations Management (15 pts)
- `CvOperationsPage.tsx` — Cardiovascular operations: CV exam scheduling (stress echo slots, MR/CCTA time blocks), protocol assignment by indication (CAD, cardiomyopathy, valve, aorta), contrast inventory tracking, stress agent inventory (dobutamine/regadenoson), turnaround time KPI cards, operator workload balancing, CV-specific revenue analytics, referring physician CV ordering patterns, patient preparation checklist management, supply chain dashboard
